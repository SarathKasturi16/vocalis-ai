AlphaBank Business Growth Loan - Official Policy

1. Qualification Requirements:
- The minimum annual turnover required to qualify is $250,000.
- The business must have been operating for at least 2 years.
- The business owner must have a credit score of 680 or higher.

2. Loan Terms & Pricing:
- The maximum loan amount available is $75,000.
- The interest rate is a fixed 7.5% APY.
- The repayment term is exactly 36 months.
- There is no early repayment penalty.

3. Required Documentation:
To apply, the applicant must submit:
- 2 years of business tax returns.
- 6 months of business bank statements.
- A valid state business license.
- A scanned copy of a government-issued ID.
